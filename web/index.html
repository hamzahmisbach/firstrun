What FirstRun can and cannot tell you

The sandbox is a Linux container with a 60 second limit and no internet; every host name resolves to a fake server that records the request and answers nothing useful. The verdict comes from fixed rules over what was observed, never from a guess. A clean result means nothing suspicious was seen in this run; it is not a promise that the command is safe on your machine.

FirstRun runs on Vultr Cloud Compute with gVisor sandboxes, Vultr Serverless Inference for unwrapping and explanations, and Vultr Object Storage for reports. Reports are unlisted and kept for a limited time. To have one removed, contact the team listed in the project README.
